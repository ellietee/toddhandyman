Who We Are

Based in Myrtle Beach, Todd Handyman Services has been helping homeowners with repairs, installations, and custom projects since 2023. We combine hands-on skill, attention to detail, and a friendly approach to make your home safer, smarter, and just a little more awesome.

Ben is our go-to craftsman, from custom woodworking to all your home repairs. With 25 years of experience as a carpenter, mechanic, and general problem-solver, he brings creativity, precision, and wisdom to every project.

Elle works behind the scenes, ensuring every project runs smoothly and no detail is overlooked. With over a decade of experience in healthcare, she understands how to make homes safer, more accessible, and easier to live in.

We’re not just about getting the job done, we’re about doing it right, on time, and with respect for your home.
